Samaritan believers

Samaritan believers · from AD 30s

Came from a distinct religious community related to Judaism, with its own temple tradition at Mount Gerizim. Acts 8 describes their acceptance of the Christian message under Philip, and the apostles’ visit from Jerusalem.
