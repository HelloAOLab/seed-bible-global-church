Bringing the confessions and catechisms into Ask

A work brief, 1 November 2026, for two people: the publishing developer (§2) and the ontology developer (§3). §1 is the shared ground; §4 is what the harness side does with the result; §5 is the handoff. It follows the ask-writings v3 spec, §4b and §7.

1. Why, and the one rule 2. Publishing developer 3. Ontology developer 4. What the harness does with it 5. Handoff and sequencing

1. Why, and the one rule

On the Christian Ask development slice, Haiku 5.5 alone beats the ask-writings harness and both reference apps. The largest single cause is a missing source. Asked Westminster Shorter Catechism 18 ("wherein consists the sinfulness of that estate whereinto man fell?"), every other system states Westminster's formulation — the guilt of Adam's first sin, the want of original righteousness, the corruption of the whole nature, and the actual transgressions that proceed from it. Ours gives a Genesis 3 study and never states it, because the library holds no Westminster, Heidelberg or 1689 text (only the Racovian Catechism) and the evidence rule forbids the model to state a teaching it cannot source. "Westminster teaches X" is proved by the catechism itself; only "these passages support X" needs the interpretive argument. We need the first without reconstructing the second.

The rule the owner set. Ask has stayed with Scripture and the writings of its own period. Bringing doctrinal sources in must not blur that: a formulation is always attributed ("Westminster teaches…"), never stated as what Scripture says; it is brought in only for the question kinds whose evidence policy allows it; and a question with no source, tradition or reader perspective stays on Scripture and its period. The layers, with WSC 18 as the worked example:

Layer WSC 18 Where it lives
Scripture and its period witnesses Gen 3; Rom 5:12–19; Eph 2:3; Ps 51:5; the LXX, the scrolls, Philo on the fall scr:, txt:, the writings domain's period layer
Doctrinal topic original sin and humanity's fallen condition sdo:Doctrine
Doctrinal formulation the particular explanation WSC 18 gives sdo:DoctrineFormulation, one per question or section, stable id
Source the catechism's exact answer, edition, location a published writing's unit: wrt:PublishedWriting, txt:Span
Theological tradition Reformed sdo:DoctrineProfile
Denominational association the bodies that adopt this catechism sdo:AdoptionAssertion — says who adopts; never supplies the teaching
Biblical interpretation the catechism's proof texts, and the readers who argue them sdo:citesPassage, sdo:throughArgument, scr:InterpretationQuestion

2.3 The unit shape — the part that matters most

One edition record per document, through scripts/writings/import.py with a new importer importers/creeds_json.py registered in IMPORTERS; source family creeds-json, release 2026-11-01, the file's sha256.

  • Catechisms (CreedFormat: Catechism; records Number/Question/Answer): one unit per question. The locator is the stable id — WSC 18, WLC 25, HC 7 (Heidelberg; put the Lord's Day in the section title), BapCat 1695 18, Keach 20. The text is Q. <question> A. <answer>, the document's own words only.
  • Proof texts go in notes, never in the text. Where a record has Proofs / AnswerWithProofs, write each lettered proof as one note ("a. Rom. 5:12, 19; Eph. 2:3"). common.place() then places the unit on those verses by note-citation, and the text stays the formulation. This is the catechism's own link from teaching to Scripture — the "biblical interpretation" layer.
  • Confessions (Chapter/Title/Sections): one unit per section, locator WCF 6.4, 2LCF 6.2, 2HC 8.3; the chapter title is the section heading. Articles (Article/Title/Content, the "Canon" format): one unit per article — BC 15; Dort's heads CD 3/4.1.
  • Creeds (a single text): one unit per clause or paragraph as the file breaks it — Nicene 1, Athanasian 3.
  • Nested catechisms (HenrysCatechism: Henry's, the Exposition, Fisher's — SubQuestions): the parent question is a unit (Fisher 18) and each sub-question a unit (Fisher 18.3) in a section named by the parent.
  • Keep the file's wording (early-modern spelling included); no modernising. No chunker — the units are the document's own divisions.

3. Ontology developer — the doctrine layer Ask can read

Most of what is needed already exists in doctrine (sdo:): sdo:Doctrine (the topic), sdo:DoctrineFormulation with formulationText / formulationSpan / statesFormulation, sdo:DoctrineProfile with inProfile, sdo:AdoptionAssertion with adopts / adoptedBy / adoptionEvidence, sdo:SourceStance, sdo:citesPassage, sdo:throughArgument, sdo:DoctrinalPolicyUse. The asks are: a projection that fills it from the published collection, a small evidence-policy vocabulary, and three new terms. Nothing here changes the project profile or RBS's concept rules.

3.1 Three new terms (T-box + C-box; shapes for each)

  • sdo:AskEvidencePolicy ⊑ at:Norm — one record per question kind: sdo:forQuestionKind (a value of a new C-box sdo:AskQuestionKind: explain-named-formulation · doctrinal-overview · bible-teaches · passage · earliest-teaching · history-of-reading · word-study · compare · factual · pastoral), and for each evidence layer (a C-box sdo:EvidenceLayer: scripture-period · formulation · tradition-profile · later-reader · denomination) a rule value from sdo:LayerRule: required · allowed · allowed-under-perspective · excluded; plus sdo:presentationOrder (the layers in the order the answer presents them) and sdo:policyRationale (exists). Shape: exactly one policy per kind; every layer named; preflight refuses a kind with none or two. The starting rows are the table in spec §4b.2.
  • sdo:evalGoldFor (datatype, on a formulation's source edition or on the formulation): the evaluation set id the source is gold for — read by the quarantine.
  • sdo:stableId on sdo:DoctrineFormulation: the locator (WSC 18, WCF 6.4) — the key the harness, the UI and the verse screen use. Confessions, catechisms, creeds and articles are told apart by sdo:formulationKind (C-box: catechism-answer · confession-section · creed-clause · article).

3.2 The projection (generated, never hand-authored)

scripts/build-doctrine-formulations.py reads the edition records whose work.kind is set (§2.4) and writes ontology/doctrine.formulations.ttl into its own graph <urn:skills:context:doctrine:formulations>:

  • one sdo:DoctrineFormulation per unit: stableId, formulationText (verbatim — public domain), formulationSpan → the published unit (wrt:PublishedWriting / txt:Span by unit id), formulationKind, formulationLanguage, sdo:citesPassage for every OSIS id in the unit's proof-text notes (this is the "biblical interpretation" edge; it is the document's own claim, so it is stated, not argued);
  • one sdo:DoctrineProfile per work.tradition value (Reformed, Reformed Baptist, Lutheran, Roman Catholic, Anglican, Eastern Orthodox, Ecumenical) — created if absent, every formulation inProfile its tradition's; the ecumenical creeds in every Christian profile;
  • sdo:ofDoctrine → an sdo:Doctrine topic, proposed by Haiku from the question/section title against the existing sdo:Doctrine list and admitted by code (an exact existing IRI or none); unresolved formulations carry no topic rather than a guessed one; reviewStatus "proposed" throughout;
  • sdo:AdoptionAssertions for denominations: a small seed table in the script (e.g. the OPC and PCA adopt the Westminster Standards; the URCNA, CRCNA and RCA the Three Forms of Unity; Reformed Baptist associations the 1689; Lutheran bodies the Augsburg Confession), each with its adoptionEvidence URL, status proposed — it names who adopts and nothing more;
  • sdo:evalGoldFor from the edition's evalGold.

Load with load_evidence_graphs.py --apply --only doctrine-formulations (add the graph to its list); preflight doctrine-formulations holds the file to a fresh build and to its shapes. The runtime never queries GraphDB per ask (GraphDB Free runs two queries at a time): the data worker needs a projection in D1 — tables doctrine_formulations (stable id, text, kind, tradition, topic, unit id, eval-gold set) and ask_evidence_policy (kind × layer → rule, order), filled by a loader beside the scripture KB's. The harness side writes the loader and the tools if you give the TTL; say which you prefer.

3.3 The competency questions (the acceptance test, as SPARQL in the data file's header)

  1. Which formulations state the doctrine original sin, in which traditions, with which proof texts? → WSC 18, WLC 25, HC 7–8, WCF 6.1–6, 2LCF 6, BC 15, with their verses.
  2. Which formulations cite Rom 5:12? → the units whose proof-text notes name it.
  3. What may an ask of kind passage bring in, and in what order? → one policy row: scripture-period required, formulation excluded unless under perspective, later readers allowed, order meaning → context → readers.
  4. Which published sources are gold for scripture-ask-catechism-wsc? → the WSC edition.
  5. Which bodies adopt the document WSC 18 belongs to? → the adoption assertions, each with its evidence URL.

Grounding reminder: every new class reaches an upper anchor (at:Norm for the policy; the formulation already reaches at:InformationArtifact), sits in exactly one semantic cluster (a new sdo:cluster_ask_policy for the three codes lists — a new codes cluster does not reuse an old cluster's IRI), and a controlled value is a skos:notation, never an IRI under sh:class. Push with sync_ontology.py push --direct doctrine, data with load_data_graph.py doctrine --apply --direct, and run python3 scripts/preflight.py --live before committing.

4. What the harness side does with it

  • Adds context.perspective to the ask (beside role); the frame step fills source, tradition, perspective, kind; a source named in the question overrides the reader's perspective.
  • Reads the evidence policy for the frame's kind and enforces it: which layers are gathered, which are allowed into the answer, in what order; a formulation is rendered with its attribution and stable id.
  • Builds the quarantine: on the independent-synthesis track the units of an evalGold source are left out of retrieval; on the source-grounded track they are open-book.
  • Runs the 2×2 on the frozen slice — {current sources, + wave A} × {current harness, draft-then-prove} — with every item labelled with its intended source or perspective, so a Westminster question is graded against Westminster and a general question never needs an unattributed Westminster answer to pass.

5. Handoff and sequencing

  1. Publishing: wave A imported, published, in the index; the slug list sent. (Blocks the experiment.)
  2. Ontology: the three terms, the policy C-box and shapes, the projection script, the loaded graph, preflight green; the TTL sent. (Blocks the policy-enforced arm, not the 2×2's first cell.)
  3. Harness: the D1 projection and tools, the frame fields, the quarantine, the 2×2.
  4. Then waves B–D and the other traditions; the policy table is revised from what the 2×2 shows, never from a single case.

Questions in either direction go through the owner; the file form of this brief is docs/bible-translation/doctrinal-collection-brief-2026-11-01.md in the skills repo.
