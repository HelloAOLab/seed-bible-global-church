How Seed Bible is built

Seed Bible is a Bible reader where everything you mark and keep lives in your own Library, not in the app's database. The app asks agents to read and write on your behalf. These pages explain how that works, in three views.

What the data means

Ontology

Every highlight, note and reading plan is a record with an agreed meaning. It is built on shared, standard vocabularies: PROV-O, P-Plan, DOLCE+DnS, the agentic-primitives substrate, and the faith and scripture ontologies.

Who talks to whom

Agentic

The app talks to two places: your Home (to sign in) and the reader's workspace agent. The workspace then talks to the other agents over A2A: your own agent, the Scripture agent, the serving plane and the publishing gateway.

The idea in four sentences

  1. You own your marks. A highlight on John 3:16 is a small document in your Library at your Home (www.faithnet.me). The app keeps none of it.
  2. The app never holds a key. When you sign in, your Home gives the reader's workspace a short-lived "ask-as-me" permission (we call it a wire). You can see and revoke it at your Home.
  3. Agents do the work. The workspace agent asks your own agent to save the mark, asks the Scripture agent for the text, and asks the serving plane what others have published.
  4. Everything has a shared meaning. Records are typed with an ontology that grounds on public standards, so any tool that knows PROV-O or the faith ontology can understand them.

Where things live

Part Address What it does
Reader app seed-reader.faithnet.io The screens you use. Works offline. Holds no key.
Home www.faithnet.me Sign-in, sign-out, contacts and friends, your Library.
Workspace agent seed-reader.workspace at seed-reader-a2a.faithnet.io The reader's backend. Coordinates every other agent.
Your agent <you>.me at a2a.faithnet.io Writes to and reads from your Library, under your permission.
Scripture agent scripture-resolver.svc at scripture.faithnet.io The one source of Bible text, signed so it can be checked.
Serving plane seed-bible.svc at seed-bible-a2a.faithnet.io An index of what people publish, for Discover and Today. Can be rebuilt from the Libraries.
